How it stays safe

  1. Your password becomes a key, on this devicePBKDF2 runs 600,000 rounds to turn it into a 256-bit key. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. The key seals the vaultEvery save locks the whole vault with AES-256-GCM, site names included.
  3. Backups stay sealed tooBackup files are encrypted the same way. Without your master password they are unreadable.

Strongroom locks itself after a few idle minutes and works with no internet connection.
